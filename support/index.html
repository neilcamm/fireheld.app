Common questions

Where are my photos? Inside the app, not in your Photos library. Kept writes them to storage that belongs to this app alone. Open Photos in the app to see them, or use Settings › Data to export them as a ZIP.

The camera says my last photo is locked. You have Face ID protection turned on and the session has expired. Tap Unlock on the viewfinder, or reopen the camera to be asked again.

The outline and auto shutter aren't showing. The guide traces your most recent photo, so it appears from your second photo onward. If it stays empty after that, check that GUIDE is on in the top bar and that Face ID protection is unlocked.

The shutter fires on its own. That's the auto shutter: line up with the outline and it counts down from five, then takes the photo. Step out of position and the count stops; move back and it starts over. Turn it off with the AUTO SHUTTER checkbox.

I missed a day. A day stays open until its cutoff hour, not midnight — the app tells you how long is left. What happens after that depends on the challenge you chose; the rules are in Settings › The rules.

How do I get my data out? Settings › Data. Your record exports as JSON and CSV, your photos as a ZIP, and your movie as a video file, all through the standard iOS share sheet.

How do I delete everything? Settings › Privacy › Delete everything. It removes the record, the photos, the widget's copy and pending reminders from your iPhone, and offers to erase the iCloud copy too. It cannot be undone — export first if you want to keep anything.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone? Your record syncs through your own iCloud account if iCloud sync is on. Photos and movies do not sync — they move with an encrypted device backup, or with an export you make yourself.

Buying Kept

Kept is one payment, once — there is no subscription, nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases on the paywall and it comes back with your Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

A note on the challenge itself

Kept records what you tell it; it doesn't give medical or fitness advice. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a demanding daily routine, and stop if you feel unwell. A streak is never a reason to keep going past what's safe.
