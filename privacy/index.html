Kept

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 August 2026

Kept has no account, no server, and no analytics. It does not send your data anywhere. Your progress photos, your journal and your challenge record are written to storage on your own iPhone that belongs to this app alone, and they stay there unless you deliberately export or share them.

What Kept collects

Nothing. The developer receives no data from this app — no personal information, no photos, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no device or advertising identifiers. There is no sign-in, no profile and no support form inside the app. Kept contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not ask for permission to track you, because it never does.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Progress photos and transformation movies — written into this app's private storage area. They are never added to your Photos library unless you choose "Save Image" or "Save Video" yourself from the iOS share sheet.
  • Your challenge record — which requirements you completed and when, the water, reading, workout and diet entries you log, and any journal text you write.
  • Your settings — reminder times, units, and whether Face ID protection is on.

Photographs are re-encoded when they are saved: orientation, size and colour profile are kept, and everything else in the image metadata is discarded — no device model, no GPS or location data, no timestamps beyond the date the app records itself. Kept does not use location services at all, and is not connected to Apple Health.

iCloud sync (optional, off-switchable)

If iCloud sync is on, your challenge record — days, entries and journal text — is mirrored into your own private iCloud database under your Apple Account, so it can follow you to another device. That storage belongs to you and is governed by Apple's terms; the developer has no server and no ability to read it.

Photos and movies are never mirrored to iCloud by the app. Only the record is. You can turn the sync off in Settings › iCloud inside the app, and "Delete everything" offers to erase the iCloud copy as well as the one on your iPhone.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone — to iCloud or to a computer — this app's data is part of that backup, including your photos, unless you turn off Include photos in backup in the app's Privacy settings. iCloud backups are encrypted in a way that Apple cannot read only if you have turned on Advanced Data Protection for your Apple Account.

Face ID and passcode

You can require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode before your photos and movie can be seen, exported or deleted. This uses Apple's authentication system, which tells the app only whether the check passed. Kept never receives or stores biometric data of any kind.

Note that journal text and your day-to-day challenge status are not behind that check, and the widget shows your day number on the Lock Screen if you add it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — when you open the camera to take a progress photo. Photos go straight into the app.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on a reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; no notification is sent from a server.
  • Face ID — only if you turn on photo protection.
  • Adding to Photos — only if you choose to save an image or video from the share sheet.

Nothing is requested at first launch, and refusing any of these leaves the rest of the app working.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your purchase is active and stores only that yes-or-no answer on your device.

Your data is yours

  • Export — your record as JSON and CSV, your photos as a ZIP, and your movie, all through the iOS share sheet.
  • Delete — "Delete everything" removes the record, the photo storage, the widget's copy, pending reminders and cached files from your iPhone, and offers to erase the iCloud copy too. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf — the controls in the app are complete.

Children

Kept is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are most likely to be clarifications rather than new uses of data.
